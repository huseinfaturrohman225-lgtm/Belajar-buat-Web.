Breed history

The Briard, which is believed to have originated in France, has been bred for centuries to
herd and to protect sheep. The breed was used by French Army as sentries,
mesengers and to search for wounded soldiers because of its fine sense of hearing.
Briads were used in the First World War almost to the point of extinction. Curently the
population of briads is slowly recovering.Charlemagne, Napoleaon, Thomas Jefferson
and Lafayette all owned briards.
